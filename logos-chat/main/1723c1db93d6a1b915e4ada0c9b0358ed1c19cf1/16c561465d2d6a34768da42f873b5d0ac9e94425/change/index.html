A tile is one seed, from the seed whose first fork moved earliest to the one whose moved latest: the tree of its run after over the tree of its run before, and over them the epochs before its first fork at both. A tree is one run, left to right by epoch from 0 to 50, the goal. The top line is the trunk. Each line below left the line it drops from at that epoch and stops at its dot. The thicker a line, the more members it ended with. The ring counts the members that never joined. A tree opens its run's page, while the site keeps one.

A round is one step of the test: the members the seed draws each add one. The two runs of a seed have the same rounds up to the one named. A run's rounds and its outcome are two records of it: two runs of the same outcome can part in a round, and two that differ can part in none.

The site keeps the runs' text and pages of the newest 11 points and no log. GitHub keeps a run's logs 90 days. Where a command finds none, the commands that run a run again are on the run's page, or on its point's where the run has none: the runs after, the runs before. What to read in a log is in the guide's Into the log.
