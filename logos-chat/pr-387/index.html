The scenarios

How many epochs a growing group of logos-chat clients lasts before it first forks.

  • saro creates the group, and the other 199 members wait to be added.
  • Each round, the seed draws one to 3 of the members that can add, and each adds the next member at once, on whatever branch it is on.
  • A member can add once it holds the group, while no member has gone past its state, unless it was refused a whole round on that state.
  • The trunk starts at saro's first state and, at each fork, follows the state more members hold or have gone past.
  • A run goes on past its first fork, and ends at the start of a round once the trunk reaches epoch 50, the trunk and the branches off it number 5 or more, all 200 members have been added, or no member can add.
  • Each member receives every frame late, by a delay of its own from 268 to 659 ms.
  • A row is a rate of loss: each member loses that share of the frames it receives, at random, Welcomes among them.
  • A column is a seed, the same at the head and at the base. At one revision a seed runs the same every time, so where a column's two trees differ, the pull request changed the run. The change may be only a re-deal of the losses: one frame more, anywhere, shifts which frames each member loses after it.

A tree is one run, left to right by epoch from 0 to 50, the goal. The top line is the trunk. Each line below left the line it drops from at that epoch and stops at its dot. The thicker a line, the more members it ended with. The ring counts the members that never joined. Hover a line for its numbers, or open a run for its labels and the command that runs it again. A strip under a row holds every seed of the rate, where a run of its group moved and the rate ran more: of each seed, how many epochs later the head's run first forked than the base's, the trunk's epoch standing in for a run that did not fork, linked to the run; under them, what the seeds came to. The fold under a strip draws the rate's other seeds as the row above draws its three.
