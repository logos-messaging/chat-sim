chat-sim asks whether a group of logos-chat clients stays one group when the network delays and
loses what its members send. A group that parts has forked: its members hold two states of the
group, and neither side can read what the other sends.

chat-sim runs every member of a group in one process, on a virtual clock, through a model of a
lossy network, and on Linux a seed gives the same run every time. Each logos-chat pull request runs
at its head and at its merge base, and the comment on it says whether the head forks earlier or
later. Each push to logos-chat's `main` runs too, and the site draws `main` over time.
