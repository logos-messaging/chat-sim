Whether a group of logos-chat clients still holds one group when the network delays and loses what
its members send. A scenario says how many members there are, what they do, what the network does to
them and what the group is expected to come to. `tools/sim` runs it with every member in one process
on a virtual clock, on Linux the same run every time from its seed. Every member writes a few one-line
records into the log, and `tools/forks` reads them and gives a verdict: `whole`, or `split at epoch 4`.
CI runs scenarios on master and on each pull request, and the site's reports show what they came to.
