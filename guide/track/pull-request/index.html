Your pull request's result

When a pull request to logos-chat opens or gets a push, logos-chat’s workflow chat-sim.yml starts chat-sim’s simulate.yml at the pull request’s head, against its merge base with main. It queues a check named chat-sim on the head and writes a running line at the top of chat-sim’s comment. A pull request whose every changed file is under docs/ or ends in .md gets its check completed as skipped. A pull request from a fork gets nothing: its workflow is handed no secrets.

The first stage runs seeds 0 to 2 of the goal, grow-waku-loss-, at 0.5, 1 and 5% loss, on GroupV2 and GroupV3: 18 runs at the head and the same 18 at the base, a job a run. Each job builds harness-run with logos-chat pinned at its side’s commit (The scenarios).

A group moved when one of its seeds has an outcome at both sides and the two differ in any field. For each group that moved, the second stage runs seeds 3 to 19 of each of its rates at both sides, six runs a job. A group where nothing moved stops at seed 2.

The verdict

Section titled “The verdict”

Each seed of a rate is a pair: the head’s run and the base’s. Its d is the head’s epochs without a fork less the base’s (How a run works). A pair is later where d is above 0, earlier where it is below, and tied where it is 0. A pair whose runs differ at the same first fork counts on neither side.

Of a rate’s k pairs later and j earlier, an exact two-sided sign test gives p: the chance of a split at least that uneven if each pair were a coin toss. A rate’s result is the first that holds:

ResultWhenMarkThe check
unbuiltchat-sim does not build at the head⚫neutral, or a failure once required
no-outcomea run at the head has no outcome⚫neutral, or a failure once required
no-baseno run at the base has an outcome⚫neutral
sameevery pair with both outcomes came out the same⚪neutral
earlierp is 1% or less, and more pairs are earlier🔴failure
laterp is 1% or less, and more pairs are later🟢success
withinthe pairs moved, within chance🟡neutral

A run at the base with no outcome leaves its pair out of the test. A group’s result is the worst of its rates, in the order unbuilt, no-outcome, earlier, later, within, no-base, same. The check fails where a group’s result fails it, else succeeds where one succeeds, so a group that forks later cannot hide one that forks earlier. With chat-sim’s repository variable CHAT_SIM_CHECK_REQUIRED set to true, unbuilt and no-outcome fail the check.

Three seeds cannot reach 1%: three pairs of three earlier give p 0.25. That is why a group that moved runs 17 seeds more. Of the pairs that moved, later or earlier, the test is beyond chance when the fewer side holds at most:

Pairs that movedThe fewer side
0 to 7never beyond chance
8 to 110
12 to 141
15 to 172
18 to 203

The check’s title says each group’s verdict in a few words: GroupV2 nothing moved in 9 runs; GroupV3 forks earlier at 1%: 0 later, 9 earlier (p 0.0039). While the runs go it reads 18 runs in progress. Its summary is the comment, and its details open the report page.

The report page

Section titled “The report page”

The comment links the pull request’s report, logos-chat/pr-N/ of the site.

PartShows
The headthe head, its merge base and chat-sim’s commit, and links to the pull request and the workflow run
The verdictsa line a group, in the comment’s words, with a chip in its result’s colour
The scenarioswhat the runs test, and how each scenario’s grow runs, from the copies of the files the run kept
The treesof each group, a row a rate and a column a seed of seeds 0 to 2: the head’s tree over the base’s, each linked to the run’s page
The stripsunder a rate that ran seeds 3 to 19, the d of each of its seeds from 0, linked to its run, and under them what the seeds came to

A tree is one run, drawn from the lineage in its run.json, left to right by epoch from 0 to 50. The top line is the trunk. Each line below left the line it drops from at that epoch and stops at its dot. The thicker a line, the more members it ended with. A ring counts the members that never joined. A run that moved is outlined. Hover a line for its numbers, or open the run (From a moved run to your machine).

The report is kept while the pull request is open. When it closes, logos-chat’s workflow has chat-sim’s Prune Pages remove logos-chat/pr-N/. A run still going then writes it again, and the weekly run of Prune Pages removes every pull request’s report that is not open.
